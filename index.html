Built for every kind of mind

MIT stands for Multi Intelligence Transforming School. We believe autism is not a barrier. It's a different way of seeing the world. Our school in Jakarta is a safe, structured and joyful space where children on the spectrum can truly flourish.

Our motto Giving HOPE & Building FUTURES

Vision

To enhance the quality of life and build meaningful futures for children with autism through independence, confidence and life skills.

Mission

To provide a transformational and holistic learning programme for children with autism to cultivate their talents and abilities.

Our curriculum weaves the mandatory school subjects (Bahasa Indonesia, English, Math and Science) together with practical daily life activities that many autistic children find challenging. From cooking to communication, every lesson has a real-world purpose.

Six reasons parents trust MIT School

What families tell us matters most once their child has settled in.

  • Individualized education plans

    Every child's goals are built around their own strengths, pace and interests, not a fixed curriculum.

  • Small, focused classes

    One teacher for every four students means real attention, every single day.

  • Autism-trained educators

    Our teachers and therapists are experienced specifically in autism support, not generalists.

  • Real-world skills

    Cooking, café service and daily routines are taught alongside academics, not as an afterthought.

  • A community, not just a school

    Family support and public events mean no parent has to navigate this alone.

  • Growth you can see

    The progress families describe, in confidence, communication and independence, speaks for itself.

    Take the first step together

    We welcome children with autism and related special needs. Our team will guide your family every step of the way.

    1. Send your enquiry

      Fill in the form and tell us a little about your child and your goals for them.

    2. Consultation meeting

      We'll arrange a warm conversation with our team to understand your child's needs and learning style.

    3. Trial visit

      Your child visits the school, meets the teachers and spends a day in our environment.

    4. Welcome to MIT School

      Once confirmed, your child officially joins our family with a personalised learning plan prepared just for them.
